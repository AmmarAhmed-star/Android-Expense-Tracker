Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Expense Tracker ("the App") handles your information. We believe in privacy first — your financial data is yours.

1. What Data We Collect

We do NOT collect any personal data. The App does NOT collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, or bank details. All expenses you enter are stored only on your device using local storage / Room Database.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

All expense entries, budget settings and preferences are stored locally on your Android device. We do not have any server to receive, store or view your data. If you uninstall the App, all local data is deleted.

3. Third-Party Services

The current version does not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDK. If we add Google AdMob in future updates, AdMob may collect anonymous device identifiers to show ads. This policy will be updated before that.

4. Permissions

The App does not ask for any sensitive permissions like Contacts, Location, SMS, or Storage (except internal app storage for its own database). No permission is used to access your personal files.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is a general utility app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy when features change. The updated date at the top will be changed. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the new policy.
